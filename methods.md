### Methods
This project analyzes gender representation among animal characters in popular English-language children's picture books [2]. Character gender was evaluated through manual reading and recorded strictly based on explicit pronouns and gender-specific text [2]. To maintain consistent data boundaries, several categories were deliberately excluded:
* **Excluded Formats**: Anthologies and story collections (e.g., *Grimm’s Fairy Tales*) [2, 3].
* **Excluded Animals**: Animals referenced only in groups (e.g., "the birds") and species names that inherently denote biological sex (e.g., cow, hen, rooster, gander) [3].
* **Excluded Visual & Implicit Cues**: Non-linguistic indicators, including illustrations, clothing, and character names [3].
* **Excluded Demographics**: Human characters, author/illustrator demographics, and sales or library circulation data [3].
* **Reader Survey Experiment**: A separate survey of 1,300 readers (`survey-animal-stories.csv`) was conducted to evaluate baseline human tendencies when assigning gender to animal prompts without textual cues [3, 4].
